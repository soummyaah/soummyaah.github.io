# Quotes I love

A collection to reuse later (site footer, about page, talk slides).

> Do the best you can until you know better. Then when you know better, do better.

— Maya Angelou

> An expert is a person who has made all the mistakes that can be made in a very narrow field.

— Niels Bohr *(attribution uncertain: commonly credited to Bohr, but no primary source is known)*

> Nothing in life is to be feared, it is only to be understood.

— Marie Curie

> Without data, you're just another person with an opinion.

— W. Edwards Deming *(attribution uncertain: widely circulated, but no source in his writings has been found)*

> To be yourself in a world that is constantly trying to make you something else is the greatest accomplishment.

— Ralph Waldo Emerson

> What you do makes a difference. You have to decide what kind of difference you want to make.

— Jane Goodall

> A ship in port is safe, but that's not what ships are built for.

— Grace Hopper

> The function of freedom is to free someone else.

— Toni Morrison

> And the day came when the risk to remain tight in a bud was more painful than the risk it took to blossom.

— Anaïs Nin

> The cure for boredom is curiosity. There is no cure for curiosity.

— Dorothy Parker

> My candle burns at both ends;
> It will not last the night;
> But, ah, my foes, and, oh, my friends —
> It gives a lovely light.

— Edna St. Vincent Millay, "First Fig" (1920). *(Often misattributed to Roald Dahl.)*
