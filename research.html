Privacy & safety in multi-agent systems

COLM 2026HAIPS workshop · oral

Benchmarking privacy leakage in multi-agent systems

Soumya Sharma, Ayana Hussain, Héber Hwang Arcolezi, Ulrich Aïvodji, Spandana Gella, Adriana Romero Soriano, Golnoosh Farnadi

pdfcodebib
tl;dr

A benchmark for what actually drives privacy leakage when LLM agents talk to each other. Across Qwen3, Llama 3.1 and Gemma models, more capable and reasoning models leak more, leakage compounds over turns, and prompt-based mitigations only go so far. Also presented at WiML.

IDEAFix: Evaluation Framework for Creative Defixation Prompting in LLMs

Florian Carichon, Soumya Sharma, Meaghan J. Girard, Romain Rampa, Golnoosh Farnadi

pdfbib
tl;dr

An evaluation framework for creativity in LLMs. It surfaces a hive-mind behaviour: different models converge on strikingly similar ideas in creative tasks. Under review at NeurIPS 2026.
