I study when AI agents should keep a secret — and when they spill it.

I’m a first-year PhD student at McGill and Mila, advised by Golnoosh Farnadi and Adriana Romero Soriano. I work on privacy and safety in multi-agent LLM systems — mostly on measuring contextual-integrity violations when agents talk to each other.

Before this I was a predoctoral researcher on the AQUA team at Google DeepMind, did my M.S. (Research) at IIT Kharagpur in the CNeRG lab, interned at Microsoft Research India, and studied CS at IIIT Delhi.

Research

My work asks what it means for an AI agent to handle information appropriately — not just whether it knows something, but whether it should pass it on, to whom, and when.

NOW

Contextual integrity for agents

Benchmarks that measure privacy leakage across multi-turn, multi-agent dialogue, and why it gets worse as models get stronger.

NOW

Robust multi-agent systems

Cooperation when some teammates may be adversarial — currently Byzantine-robust multi-agent RL.

Financial & clinical NLP
