Soumya Sharma

PhD student · EQUAL Lab, McGill & Mila · Montréal

I study when AI agents should keep a secret — and when they spill it.

I’m a PhD student at McGill and Mila in the EQUAL Lab, advised by Golnoosh Farnadi and Adriana Romero Soriano. I research safety, privacy and alignment in multi-agent LLM systems: benchmarks that show when agents leak what they shouldn’t, and evaluations that hold up.

Before this I spent two years as a predoctoral researcher on the AQUA team at Google DeepMind India, working on reward modeling, evaluation and self-improving agents. Earlier: an M.S. (Research) at IIT Kharagpur with Pawan Goyal and Niloy Ganguly, a research internship at Microsoft Research India, and a B.Tech at IIIT Delhi.

Research

all papers & tl;drs →

My work asks what it means for an AI agent to handle information appropriately — not just whether it knows something, but whether it should pass it on, to whom, and when.

NOW

Privacy in multi-agent LLMs

Benchmarks and adversarial probing for contextual-integrity violations when agents talk to agents — and to people whose data implicates others.

NOW

Alignment & evaluation

Reward models robust to spurious correlations, dimension-wise raters, and evaluations of creativity that catch models converging on the same ideas.

BEFORE

Financial & clinical NLP

Datasets and benchmarks for financial documents with Goldman Sachs, and knowledge-graph-grounded medical NLI, at ACL, EMNLP and WWW.
